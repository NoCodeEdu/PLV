Drop a playlist exported from Apple Music or iTunes and save it as a CSV or Excel file. Everything runs in your browser; the file never leaves your device.

How to export a playlist from Music or iTunes
  1. Select the playlist in the sidebar.
  2. Choose File › Library › Export Playlist…
  3. Pick Unicode Text (the most columns) or XML. Exporting the whole library as XML lets you pick any playlist here, or save them all as sheets in one workbook.
